===================================================================
CP468 Project Demonstration: Seq2Seq LSTM vs. Flan-T5 Baseline
===================================================================

1. Project Overview:
   - Task: Abstractive Summarization / Headline Generation
   - Dataset: CNN/DailyMail (10k Train / 1k Val / 1k Test)

2. Models Compared:
   - Custom Seq2Seq LSTM with Bahdanau Attention (27,617,088 parameters)
   - Local LLM Baseline: google/flan-t5-base (~250M parameters)

3. Key Training Results (Custom LSTM):
   - Epoch 1 Loss: 6.2946
   - Epoch 5 Loss: 3.8209
   - Saved Weights: checkpoints/lstm_seq2seq.pt

4. Core Findings:
   - Flan-T5 produces higher semantic coherence and fluency.
   - Custom LSTM captures general topic structure but exhibits 
     higher sensitivity to unseen vocabulary.
